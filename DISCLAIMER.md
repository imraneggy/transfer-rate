# Disclaimer

The rates shown in this app and JSON feed are **indicative**, sourced from
publicly displayed rate pages of third-party remittance providers. They are
provided for general information only.

* Rates may differ from what you actually receive at the provider —
  especially for amounts, channels, or products not matched by our
  default scrape (AED 1,000, online transfer, bank deposit).
* Rates may be **stale**: they are refreshed every 15 minutes at best, and
  may be older if a provider's site is unreachable.
* Some rates are **gross** (before fees) and some are **effective** (after
  fees). The app distinguishes where it can; trust the provider's own
  quote screen before sending money.
* This is **not financial advice**. Confirm at the provider before
  initiating a transfer.
* This project is **not affiliated** with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any
  of the providers listed. Provider names are used for nominative
  reference only.
