# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Summary

* Be respectful and welcoming.
* Disagreement on technical points is fine; personal attacks are not.
* Harassment of any kind is unacceptable.
* Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code, and contributions that
  violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors
  for repeated or severe violations.

## Reporting

Open a private GitHub issue or contact the maintainer directly. All
reports are treated confidentially. We will respond within 7 days.
